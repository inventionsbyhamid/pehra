## Try it

* Live demo: {{link.demo}}. Press Try the demo and you get a private sandbox society for a day, no sign in. It opens
  the control room with two demo cameras, Plaza (OpenCV's sample clip) and Pool (a Pexels stock clip), and an open critical incident, with the Telegram
  message the society's group would get. Add your laptop or phone as a camera from the demo bar, type a watch such as
  "someone in front of the camera", and see it fire.
* Video: {{link.video}}
* Code: {{link.repo}}

The live demo runs on our own server, with stock OpenCV and an OpenRouter model.
